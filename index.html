These six instruments own the concepts the rest of the series applies. Sector frameworks, facility-class design bases, application guides and vendor companions apply them by reference; they do not define them. Published as a dated edition — a coherence claim that these instruments, at these versions, were reconciled against one another as of the edition date.

FD-BL — The Bright Line

Version 0.3 · 2026-08 Foundational definition

The placement criterion. The Bright Line is drawn on the consequence of the individual action, assessed per action — not on the criticality tier or the technology class.

FD-LD — Layer Decomposition and Determinism

Version 0.1 · 2026-08 Foundational definition

Withdraws the fused doctrine that read determinism as placement. Determinism decides scope — whether a component is subject to Bright Line analysis at all; consequence decides placement.

FD-BR — The Broker

Version 0.1 · 2026-08 Foundational definition
