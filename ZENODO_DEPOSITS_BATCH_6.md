**Description** (paste as written):

The fourth edition of the Foundational Definitions, the instruments that own the concepts the Design Basis for AI Governance in Critical Infrastructure corpus applies. Its members are the Bright Line (FD-BL v0.6), Layer Decomposition and Determinism (FD-LD v0.5), the Envelope (FD-EV v0.2), the Broker (FD-BR v0.11), Mode of Discharge (FD-BL-D1 v0.5), Indication Integrity (FD-BL-D2 v0.5), Local Sovereignty (FD-LS v0.6) and, new in this edition, Design Basis Layers (FD-DBL v0.16), with a dated copy of the Governing Stack Register (FD-REG v0.33) as a companion. Design Basis Layers defines the three layers of design basis analysis on which the corpus rests: the Design Basis layer, the accident layer and the adversary layer. It defines the service function that holds them together, the three assumptions of the accident layer, and the four characteristics, intentionality, persistence, precision and denial, all of which an actor must show to reach the adversary layer. It also states the change in what success means at that layer: the facility reaches and holds a protected state using only resources present at it. Three existing members take in the author's answers to questions the sector work put to the family between 8 and 14 October 2026. The Bright Line states how a vendor's release, a staged release and a change to an enterprise network are placed. Layer Decomposition and Determinism states that the carve-out does not cover a component running a configuration other than the one formally tested. Indication Integrity states that a correct indication can be insufficient. Placement remains consequence, action by action, throughout. The edition is a dated coherence claim: the eight instruments were read against one another as of 19 October 2026, and every cross-reference between them resolves. It does not assert that the wider corpus has been conformed to them as of this date. The surveillance branch (FD-SBL) is not included; it is held from release pending external review.
